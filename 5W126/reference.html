The Seven Scheduling Laws

  1. Law 1

    Materials before experts — no expert is called until materials are staged and predecessors done.

    材料先行:专家进场前,材料就位、前置完成。

  2. Law 2

    Measure the real thing — no rough-in until the governing fixture is on site and measured.

    实物量尺:洁具不到场不定位。

  3. Law 3

    Batch the scarce trade — multi-day continuous batches, never day-bounced.

    批量成势:稀缺工种连续多日批,不跨场跳。

  4. Law 4

    Label and hand off — veterans label everything; helpers continue the thread.

    标注接力:师傅标号走人,帮手接尾。

  5. Law 5

    Anchor / Sponge / Batch — F2 anchor, F1 duplex sponge, Astoria batch site.

    锚/海绵/批量场。

  6. Law 6

    Sequence out the rework — covers after tests, LVP after cabinets, tile before toilets.

    把返工排出去。

  7. Law 7

    Green-light dispatch — predecessors done + materials staged, or don't call.

    绿灯派工:不绿灯不叫人。
